Privacy notice

What this site stores about you.

The site is hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google), which keeps standard server logs such as IP address and browser. If you create an account, Firebase Authentication stores your email address (and your Google name and photo if you sign in with Google).

Your language choice is stored in your browser (localStorage). Nothing else is collected.

Accounts hold no profile data yet. Before profiles open, this notice will be replaced by a full KVKK disclosure. You can delete your account at any time on the account page.
