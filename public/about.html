About

Who is behind this, and why.

One person, for now

Right now this is one person. This site is a modest start: I am compiling my own notes — places I have seen, communities I have visited, sources I learn from — so that a roadmap, and later a project group, can grow out of them.

Who I am

Ertuğrul Yıldırım, a software developer living in Fethiye, Muğla. I hike, camp and practise rural life, and I started the open-source Collective Bucket initiative.

Why

Modern work leaves little room for making things together. I want to find out whether a small community can combine workshops, social sciences, art and shared living without exploitation or hierarchy.

What I bring

Software and open-source practice, and the time to keep these notes in order.

What I lack

Legal and cooperative expertise, construction and workshop skills, land, capital and co-founders. The contribution guide lists these as open roles.

Contact
