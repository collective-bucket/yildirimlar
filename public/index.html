Notes toward a cooperative in rural Muğla: workshops, social sciences, art and shared living, built on sweat equity and decisions by consent.

Right now this is one person. This site is a modest start: I am compiling my own notes — places I have seen, communities I have visited, sources I learn from — so that a roadmap, and later a project group, can grow out of them.
