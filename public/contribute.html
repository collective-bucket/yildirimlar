Contribution guide

What the project needs, how decisions are made, and how to start.

Right now I am alone, so every role below is open. Nobody is recruited for a title: you say what you can bring, and roles change only by consent.

Roles we need

Open questions (help wanted)

  • Cooperative type: production, housing or agricultural development. Founding a cooperative needs at least seven partners (Law No. 1163).
  • How to define sweat equity in the articles of association and internal rules; the law has no direct concept for it.
  • Land: zoning status, agricultural land restrictions, building permits and accommodation rules.
  • Tax, insurance and social security in shared living and shared work.
  • Personal data: KVKK notice and how visible member profiles should be.
  • Practice with consent-based decisions and conflict resolution.

Ways to contribute

  • Time and labour
  • Skills
  • Knowledge, especially of other communities
  • Network
  • Limited capital, which never buys decision weight

Sweat equity

Contributions are recorded and visible. They are the basis for ownership later; money is not.

Decisions

Sociocracy and consent. Anyone can object, but an objection needs a reason and a proposal to resolve it.

Code of conduct

No exploitation, no building private hierarchies, no sharing other people's personal data.

Commitment levels

  • Observer: follow and comment
  • Remote: a few hours a month
  • Seasonal: on site for periods
  • Full time

Volunteer notebook

Until there are enough people for discussions on this site, volunteers work in a shared notebook: open the notebook.

How to start

  • Read the vision and the roadmap.
  • Write in the volunteer notebook which role or open question you can help with.
  • Accounts and member profiles come next; you will be able to register here.
